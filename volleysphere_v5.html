Welcome to VolleySphere

One connected platform for club operations, coaching, performance and player development. VolleySphere aims to make the everyday work of a volleyball club easier to organise, understand and improve.

Our purpose

Bringing every part of the volleyball journey together

From managing teams and coordinating staff to recording training and match performance, following physical development and reviewing emerging talent, VolleySphere is designed to connect useful information in one clear, user-friendly space. Different people have different responsibilities, so each role has its own dedicated portal.

Portal introduction · Club leadership

Management Portal

A central workspace for club directors and administrators to understand and coordinate the organisation as a whole.

What can management do?

The Management Portal is intended to give club leadership a clear picture of teams, people, registrations and operational activity, helping directors coordinate resources and make informed decisions.

Teams and seasons

Organise teams, squads, season information and coaching assignments.

Players and registrations

Keep player records and registration workflows organised.

Staff and responsibilities

Coordinate coaches, technical staff and their responsibilities.

Club-wide overview

Review schedules, activity summaries and reports across the club.

Portal introduction · Coaching and performance

Technical Staff Portal

A practical workspace for the people who plan training, guide athletes, evaluate performance and support team development.

What can technical staff do?

The Technical Staff Portal brings day-to-day coaching workflows together. Coaches and authorised staff can record activities, review performance and build a clearer picture of player and team progress.

Attendance

Record session participation and review attendance patterns.

Training centre

Capture selected technical indicators during drills and follow progress.

Match centre

Log match actions and review team and player statistics.

Physical assessment

Record structured tests and compare results over time.

Talent scouting

Review submitted profiles and document scouting observations.

Reports and insights

Bring records together to support coaching discussions and decisions.

Portal introduction · Athlete experience

Player Portal

A personal space where players can understand their activities, follow their progress and stay connected with their team.

What could players access?

The Player Portal is designed to make relevant information easy to find, helping each athlete take an active role in their development while keeping personal information appropriately protected.

My profile

View personal sporting information, team and playing position.

My schedule

Check upcoming training sessions, matches and team activities.

My attendance

Review personal attendance history and session participation.

My performance

See coach-approved training and match feedback relevant to the player.

My physical progress

Follow authorised assessment results and development over time.

My goals

Understand agreed development priorities and next steps with the coach.

Player accounts, personal dashboards and privacy permissions are planned for a future secure version. This prototype does not provide individual player logins.

What a player profile could contain

Only information relevant to evaluation
Player basics

Name, age group, country, position, height and playing history.

Evidence

Short match or training clips, competition level and verified references.

Scout evaluation

Technical observations, physical context, potential fit and next action.

Important safeguards: for minors, obtain appropriate parent/guardian consent before collecting profiles or videos. Limit who can view personal information, avoid public exposure of private data, define retention and deletion rules, and check eligibility/transfer rules before any recruitment decision. Video is evidence for human review, not an automatic verdict.

What a coach can learn

  • Which players have missed several sessions?
  • Is serve accuracy improving across recent training sessions?
  • Which match skills produced the most errors?
  • How are physical test results changing over time?

A real report requires consistent definitions, enough observations and coach context. A percentage alone is not a full performance evaluation.
